# Kestrel Analytics — Billing and Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Policy owner: Finance · Audience: customers and support staff

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how Kestrel Analytics charges for its plans, how invoices are issued, and when refunds are possible. It applies to all paid plans: Starter, Team, Business and Enterprise. Enterprise contracts may contain different terms; when they do, the contract takes precedence over this policy. Current prices are published on the Kestrel pricing page and are not repeated here.

## 2. Billing cycles

Paid plans are billed per seat, either monthly or annually. Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the original purchase. Annual plans are charged once a year, on the anniversary of the purchase.

Customers who choose annual billing receive a 15% discount compared with twelve months of monthly billing. Switching from monthly to annual billing is possible at any time and takes effect immediately; the unused part of the current month is credited. Switching from annual to monthly billing takes effect at the next renewal.

## 3. Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Business and Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice, with payment due within 30 days of the invoice date.

The card on file can be updated by the workspace Owner from **Settings → Plan → Payment method**. We do not store full card numbers; they are held by our payment processor.

## 4. Invoices

An invoice is issued for every charge and sent by e-mail to the billing contact of the workspace. Invoices can also be downloaded from **Settings → Plan → Invoices**. The billing contact and the company details printed on invoices (name, address, VAT number) can be changed by the Owner; changes apply to future invoices only. A corrected copy of a past invoice can be requested from support within 90 days of its date.

## 5. Adding and removing seats

Seats can be added at any time. Added seats are charged immediately, pro-rated to the end of the current billing period.

Seats can be removed at any time, but the reduction takes effect at the next renewal. No credit is given for removed seats during the current period.

## 6. Refunds

**Monthly plans** are not refundable. When a monthly plan is cancelled, it remains active until the end of the month already paid.

**Annual plans** can be refunded in full if the request is made within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable.

Refund requests must be sent by the workspace Owner to billing@kestrel.example. Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days and returned to the original payment method.

Charges caused by a billing error on our side (for example a duplicate charge) are always refunded in full, whatever the plan and whatever the date.

## 7. Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry the card automatically 3 days and 7 days after the first attempt, and the workspace Owner receives an e-mail after each failed attempt.

If the payment still fails, the workspace has a grace period of 14 days from the first failed attempt. During the grace period everything keeps working. After the grace period, the workspace becomes read-only: members can view dashboards but cannot edit them, and datasets stop refreshing.

A read-only workspace returns to normal as soon as the outstanding amount is paid.

## 8. Cancellation

The Owner can cancel a paid plan from **Settings → Plan → Cancel plan**. Cancellation stops future charges. The workspace stays on its paid plan until the end of the period already paid, then moves to the Free plan. Content above the Free plan limits becomes read-only.

## 9. Taxes

Prices are shown without tax. VAT or sales tax is added according to the billing address. Business customers in the European Union who provide a valid VAT number are invoiced without VAT under the reverse-charge mechanism.

## 10. Contact

Billing questions: billing@kestrel.example. Please include the workspace name and, if relevant, the invoice number.
