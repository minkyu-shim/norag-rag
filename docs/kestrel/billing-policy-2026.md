# Kestrel Analytics — Billing and Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 March 2026 · Policy owner: Finance · Audience: customers and support staff

This policy replaces the Billing and Refund Policy effective 1 January 2025. Requests made before 1 March 2026 are handled under the previous policy.

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how Kestrel Analytics charges for its plans, how invoices are issued, and when refunds are possible. It applies to all paid plans: Starter, Team, Business and Enterprise. Enterprise contracts may contain different terms; when they do, the contract takes precedence over this policy. Current prices are published on the Kestrel pricing page and are not repeated here.

## 2. Billing cycles

Paid plans are billed per seat, either monthly or annually. Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the original purchase. Annual plans are charged once a year, on the anniversary of the purchase.

Customers who choose annual billing receive a 20% discount compared with twelve months of monthly billing. Switching from monthly to annual billing is possible at any time and takes effect immediately; the unused part of the current month is credited. Switching from annual to monthly billing takes effect at the next renewal.

## 3. Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and SEPA direct debit for customers in the euro area. Team, Business and Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice, with payment due within 30 days of the invoice date.

The payment method can be updated by the workspace Owner from **Settings → Plan → Payment method**. We do not store full card or account numbers; they are held by our payment processor.

## 4. Invoices

An invoice is issued for every charge and sent by e-mail to the billing contact of the workspace. Invoices can also be downloaded from **Settings → Plan → Invoices**. The billing contact and the company details printed on invoices (name, address, VAT number) can be changed by the Owner; changes apply to future invoices only. A corrected copy of a past invoice can be requested from support within 12 months of its date.

## 5. Adding and removing seats

Seats can be added at any time. Added seats are charged immediately, pro-rated to the end of the current billing period.

Seats can be removed at any time. On monthly plans the reduction takes effect at the next monthly charge. On annual plans the reduction takes effect at the next renewal, and no credit is given for the current period.

## 6. Refunds

**Monthly plans** are not refundable. When a monthly plan is cancelled, it remains active until the end of the month already paid.

**Annual plans** can be refunded if the request is made within 60 days of the purchase or renewal date. The refund is pro-rated from the date of the request: you are refunded the unused full months of the annual period. After 60 days, annual plans are not refundable.

Refund requests must be sent by the workspace Owner, either from **Settings → Plan → Request a refund** or by e-mail to billing@kestrel.example. Approved refunds are processed within 14 days and returned to the original payment method.

Charges caused by a billing error on our side (for example a duplicate charge) are always refunded in full, whatever the plan and whatever the date.

## 7. Failed payments

When a payment fails, the workspace Owner and the billing contact receive an e-mail straight away. We then retry the payment automatically 1 day, 3 days and 7 days after the first attempt.

Each retry uses the payment method on file at the time of the retry, so updating the card before the next retry is usually enough to solve the problem. No late fee is charged.

If all retries fail, the workspace enters a grace period of 21 days from the first failed attempt, during which everything keeps working. A banner reminds Admins that payment is overdue.

After the grace period, the workspace becomes read-only: members can view dashboards but cannot edit them, scheduled reports are paused, and datasets stop refreshing.

If the amount is still unpaid 60 days after the first failed attempt, the workspace is suspended: nobody can sign in to it until the outstanding amount is paid. A suspended workspace is not deleted, and returns to normal as soon as the payment succeeds.

## 8. Cancellation

The Owner can cancel a paid plan from **Settings → Plan → Cancel plan**. Cancellation stops future charges. The workspace stays on its paid plan until the end of the period already paid, then moves to the Free plan. Content above the Free plan limits becomes read-only.

Cancelling an annual plan does not by itself trigger a refund. To be refunded, request it as described in section 6.

## 9. Taxes

Prices are shown without tax. VAT or sales tax is added according to the billing address. Business customers in the European Union who provide a valid VAT number are invoiced without VAT under the reverse-charge mechanism.

## 10. Contact

Billing questions: billing@kestrel.example. Please include the workspace name and, if relevant, the invoice number.
